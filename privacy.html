LEGAL & TRANSPARENCY

PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: September 2026 • We respect your privacy. No shady data harvesting, no spam.

1 Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy outlines how Builtbyus ("we", "our", "us") collects, utilizes, and protects information when you browse our website (builtbyus.dev), submit project inquiries, or interact with our digital services.

2 Information We Collect

A. Direct Inquiries: When you submit our "Start a Project" modal or reach out via email/WhatsApp, we collect your name, business email address, phone number, company name, project brief, and budget range.

B. Technical & Device Data: Like most web servers, we temporarily log anonymized technical details such as your browser user agent, IP address, referral URL, and operating system to monitor uptime, prevent cyber threats, and optimize page load speed.

C. Cookies: We only use essential cookies for user preferences (such as remembering your cookie consent choice and session interactions). We do not deploy third-party advertising trackers.

3 How We Use Your Data

  • To prepare project proposals, scopes of work, and contract agreements.
  • To communicate project milestones, updates, and schedule sprint reviews.
  • To prevent automated form abuse, spam, and DDoS attacks.
  • To improve website performance, responsiveness, and user experience.

4 Data Security & Retention

All web transmission is encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS protocols with HSTS preloading. We store project records and client communications in secure, access-controlled environments. We retain client data only as long as necessary to fulfill contractual agreements and statutory accounting obligations.

5 Your Rights & Choices

You have the right to request access to any personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request complete deletion ("Right to be Forgotten"). To exercise your rights, simply send an email to privacy@builtbyus.dev.
